About

I run DesignPraise Studio in Lagos. I direct and design motion, edit and finish films, animate in 2D and 3D, and write the scripts and plugins that make production faster.

My background is in electronic engineering, so I approach creative work as a system: clear rules, reusable templates and tools that remove repetitive work. I also teach film production and mentor students through their short films.
